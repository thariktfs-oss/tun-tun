Rental Terms & Conditions

Please read the following information carefully before making a vehicle rental booking.

Please Note: The following terms are proposed for the SwiftDrive Rentals website assignment. They should be reviewed and approved by the business before being used as an actual rental agreement.

These terms explain the proposed conditions for booking and renting vehicles through SwiftDrive Rentals. By submitting a booking request, customers should review and agree to the applicable rental conditions before the rental is confirmed.

01

Eligibility Requirements

Customers must meet the applicable legal age requirements and hold a valid driving licence suitable for the selected vehicle.

  • Customers must provide accurate personal information.
  • A valid driving licence must be presented when required.
  • Additional eligibility checks may apply depending on the vehicle.
  • The rental company may verify documents before handing over a vehicle.
02

Booking Policy

Customers can select a vehicle, enter their pickup and return dates, and submit a booking request through the website.

  • All booking information must be correct and complete.
  • Submitting a request does not automatically guarantee availability.
  • A booking is confirmed only after the rental company approves it.
  • Customers should review the vehicle details and rental period before submitting a request.
03

Required Documents

Customers may be asked to provide the following documents before collecting the vehicle:

  • A valid national identity card or passport.
  • A valid driving licence and any legally required additional permit.
  • Contact details for rental communication.
  • Any additional documents reasonably required under the approved rental policy.
04

Rental Charges and Payment

The applicable rental charges should be displayed or communicated to the customer before the booking is finalised.

  • Rental charges depend on the selected vehicle and rental duration.
  • Any deposit, additional fee or applicable tax should be explained before payment.
  • Accepted payment methods should be confirmed by the rental company.
  • Customers should obtain a receipt or payment confirmation where applicable.
05

Vehicle Pickup and Return

Customers should follow the agreed pickup and return arrangements stated in their confirmed booking.

  • Customers should arrive at the agreed pickup location on time.
  • The vehicle's condition and existing damage should be checked before departure.
  • The vehicle should be returned at the agreed time and location.
  • Late returns may result in additional charges if these were disclosed in the approved rental agreement.
06

Vehicle Usage

Customers must operate the rented vehicle responsibly and comply with applicable laws.

  • The vehicle must be driven only by authorised drivers.
  • Traffic laws and road safety requirements must be followed.
  • The vehicle must not be used for unlawful activities.
  • Customers must not sub-rent or transfer the vehicle without permission.
  • Smoking, pets and travel outside approved areas may be subject to the agreed rental policy.
07

Cancellation and Changes

Customers who need to cancel or change a booking should contact the rental company as early as possible.

  • Booking changes depend on vehicle availability.
  • Any cancellation deadline and refund conditions must be communicated before confirmation.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the agreed cancellation policy and applicable law.
  • The rental company should notify customers if it cannot fulfil a confirmed booking.
08

Damage, Accidents and Breakdowns

Customers should report accidents, damage, theft or mechanical problems to the rental company as soon as reasonably possible.

  • Customers should follow applicable legal and emergency procedures.
  • Accidents should be reported to the relevant authorities when required by law.
  • Customers should not authorise major repairs without the rental company's approval, except where immediate action is legally required for safety.
  • Insurance coverage, excess amounts and responsibility for damage must be explained in the final rental agreement.
09

Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for using the vehicle carefully and following the agreed rental conditions.

  • Provide truthful and up-to-date information.
  • Keep the vehicle secure and take reasonable care of it.
  • Return the vehicle with its accessories and documents where applicable.
  • Pay agreed charges and any properly established additional fees.
  • Inform the rental company about changes that may affect the booking.
10

Questions About These Terms

If you have questions about a booking or a rental condition, please contact the SwiftDrive Rentals team for clarification before confirming your rental.
